2. The Short Version

Chetana scans and forgets.
  • No accounts. No login. No sign-up.
  • Your scans are checked and not stored.
  • Your scam scans stay on local models on our servers. Chat is local-first and may use bounded Anthropic or OpenAI fallback if local chat models do not respond.
  • Voice notes are sent only after you tap Ask Chetana, transcribed on our host, and the raw audio is deleted before the response returns.
  • We do not require an account or build a personal profile. A scan can still contain personal information that you choose to submit.
  • We track only anonymous scan counts (e.g., "400 scans today") for service health.
  • Your browser may keep private hashed thread hints so repeated UPI IDs, phone numbers, or links can be recognized on your device.

3. What Data We Collect

3.1 During a Scan (Not Stored)

When you submit a message, link, UPI ID, or phone number for checking:

  • Your submission is processed in real time by our local AI models (Ollama) and pattern-matching engine
  • A risk result is generated and shown to you
  • The submitted content and the result are not saved after the response is sent
  • No logs of individual scans are kept

3.2 During a Voice Check (Raw Audio Not Stored)

When you record a short voice note and tap Ask Chetana:

  • Your browser uploads the voice note to Chetana over HTTPS after that explicit action
  • The note is limited to 30 seconds and 8 MB, decoded in a private temporary directory, and transcribed by a local whisper.cpp model on Chetana's host
  • No external AI provider receives the audio
  • The raw audio and temporary files are deleted before the transcription response is returned
  • The transcript is checked like typed scan text and is not retained by the voice transcriber

3.3 During Chat (Not Stored by Chetana)

When you send a chat message for guidance:

  • Chetana tries local allowlisted models first
  • If the operator has enabled fallback and the local chat ladder does not respond in time, the chat message may be sent to Anthropic or OpenAI to produce a reply
  • Gemini is not used for Chetana chat
  • Chetana does not retain the chat content after the response is sent

3.4 Anonymous Analytics (Aggregated Only)

What We Track Example Contains PII?
Total scan count "1,247 scans today" No
Scan type breakdown "60% messages, 25% links, 15% UPI" No
Language usage "40% Hindi, 30% English" No
Risk level distribution "22% flagged as high risk" No

These are aggregate counters only. They cannot be traced back to any individual user or scan.

3.5 Local Browser Thread Hints (On Your Device)

Chetana can remember whether a UPI ID, phone number, or link domain appeared in another scan on the same browser. This is done with SHA-256 hashes in your browser's localStorage. The server does not receive your thread history, thread IDs, or local identifier index.

  • Raw scanned text is not saved in this thread store
  • Raw UPI IDs, phone numbers, and links are not saved in this thread store
  • Thread hints expire automatically after 30 days
  • You can delete them anytime by clearing site data for chetana.activemirror.ai
Clear local scan memory

Removes repeated-scan hints, local counters, queued scan events, old scan history, and vigilance receipts from this browser. It keeps language, install, consent, senior mode, and family settings.

5. No Aadhaar Data

Chetana does not accept Aadhaar data. Do not submit your Aadhaar number. If an Aadhaar number appears in text you submit for scanning, it is not extracted, stored, or processed separately.

6. How Your Data Is Processed

When you submit something for scanning:

  1. Your submitted text, screenshot, or consented voice note reaches our server over an encrypted connection (HTTPS)
  2. Our local AI models (Ollama, running on our own infrastructure) and pattern-matching rules analyze scam scans
  3. Chat uses a local-first allowlisted model ladder and only falls back to Anthropic or OpenAI if that operator setting is enabled and the local chat ladder does not respond
  4. A risk score and explanation are generated
  5. The result is sent back to you
  6. Submitted content and results are discarded — not written to an individual scan database or content log. Raw voice audio is deleted before the transcription response returns.

Core scam scan analysis stays on Chetana's own infrastructure. If chat fallback is active, the specific chat message may be sent to Anthropic or OpenAI for that reply. Gemini is excluded from Chetana chat.

10. Your Rights Under DPDP Act 2023

As a Data Principal under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following rights:

10.1 Right to Access (Section 11)

You can ask us what data we hold about you. Since we do not store scan data or personal information, the answer in most cases will be: nothing.

To make a request, email paul@activemirror.ai.

10.2 Right to Erasure (Section 13)

You can request deletion of any data we hold about you. Since scan data is not stored, there is generally nothing to delete. If you have used the Telegram bot and want your Telegram-side conversation removed, you can delete the chat on Telegram directly.

10.3 Right to Withdraw Consent

You consent to processing by submitting a scan. You can withdraw at any time by simply not using the service. No data persists after you stop.

10.4 Right to Correction (Section 12)

If any data we hold about you is incorrect, contact us at paul@activemirror.ai and we will correct it.

10.5 Right to Grievance Redressal (Section 14)

If you have a complaint about how we handle your data:

  1. Email paul@activemirror.ai
  2. We will respond within 30 days
  3. If you are not satisfied, you may escalate through any channel available under Indian law

10.6 Right to Nominate (Section 14(2))

You may nominate another person to exercise your data rights in case of death or incapacity. Contact us to register a nominee.

11. Children's Data (DPDP Act Section 9)

Chetana is intended for users aged 18 and above.

  • We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18
  • We do not have age verification — parental supervision is advised
  • We do not track, profile, or target any user
  • If you believe a child has submitted data, contact us for immediate deletion

12. Data Security (IT Act Section 43A)

  • All connections to Chetana use HTTPS encryption
  • Core scam scan processing happens on our own infrastructure in India
  • Optional chat fallback is limited to bounded Anthropic or OpenAI replies when the local chat ladder does not respond
  • No user data is stored in cloud databases
  • No remote access to any stored data

13. Cross-Border Data Transfer

Chetana processes all scam scan data on servers in India. Cloudflare (CDN) may route your web request through servers outside India as part of standard internet delivery. If operator-enabled chat fallback is active, the specific chat message for that reply may be processed by Anthropic or OpenAI outside India. Gemini is not used for Chetana chat.
