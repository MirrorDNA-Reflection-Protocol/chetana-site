# Chetana x Government of Goa

## One-line pitch

Make Goa the first proving ground for a public, privacy-preserving scam-pause layer that helps citizens stop, verify, and reach official help before or immediately after a digital scam.

## The 10-minute meeting

### 0:00-1:00 - The problem

Scam response begins after a citizen has already clicked, paid, shared an OTP, or installed an app. Goa is strengthening the official response layer. Chetana adds a simple citizen-side prevention and routing layer before that point.

Official historical context: MHA reported 6,052 cyber-fraud incidents and Rs 149.06 crore reported in Goa from NCRP inception through 28 February 2025. These are cumulative official figures, not current incidence or Chetana performance.

### 1:00-3:00 - Show, do not explain

1. Open `https://chetana.activemirror.ai`.
2. Upload the controlled scam screenshot.
3. Point to the plain-language verdict and one immediate action.
4. Point to 1930, cybercrime.gov.in, and Chakshu.
5. Show that no login was required and the assessment receipt was signed.

### 3:00-5:00 - Why Goa

- Chetana is built in Goa.
- Goa's 2025 Startup Policy includes cybersecurity and AI.
- Goa's Open Innovation Challenge explicitly supports pilots with government and industry.
- Goa Police launched E-Zero FIR for cyber-financial fraud and PRISM in February 2026.
- The first pilot requires no access to police, bank, or government systems.

### 5:00-7:00 - The offer

Run a 30-day pilot for one audience and one distribution channel:

- Audience: senior citizens, women, students, small merchants, or government employees.
- Distribution: one source-tagged QR/link through an existing awareness channel.
- Product: screenshot, text, voice, payment-proof, and tap-only scam checks.
- Reporting: aggregate PilotTrace measures; no sponsor-visible raw scam content by default.
- Closeout: one written scale, revise, or stop recommendation.

### 7:00-9:00 - What gets measured

- Qualifying scans and unique sessions.
- High-risk pauses.
- Official-rail taps and follow-through sessions.
- Languages used.
- Case or evidence packets saved.
- False-safe complaints and false-alarm reports.
- Invalid, duplicate, synthetic, and out-of-window rows.
- Privacy controls used.

Do not promise fraud-loss reduction, recovery rates, accuracy, or complaint-volume reduction before a real pilot establishes them.

### 9:00-10:00 - The ask

Ask for four decisions:

1. Name one Government of Goa department owner.
2. Name one pilot audience and one distribution channel.
3. Approve the official escalation wording and privacy boundary.
4. Agree to review a written result after 30 days and decide whether to scale, revise, or stop.

## Strong answers to likely objections

### Is this replacing 1930 or Goa Police?

No. Chetana sits before and beside official systems. It helps citizens pause earlier and routes urgent cases to 1930 and other official rails.

### Does government receive citizen screenshots or messages?

Not by default. Sponsor reporting is aggregate. Raw examples require a separate, explicit consent and research contract.

### Does it require integration?

No for the first pilot. A source-tagged link or QR is enough. Any later integration requires separate security, legal, data-processing, and procurement review.

### Is it proven to reduce fraud?

Not yet. The pilot is designed to test use, pauses, handoffs, errors, and operational fit. Chetana does not claim population-level efficacy.

### Why not just run another awareness campaign?

Awareness tells people what scams look like. Chetana gives them somewhere to act in the exact moment of doubt and measures whether they pause and reach the correct rail.

### What makes it Goa-specific?

It is built in Goa, can launch through Goa channels, routes to India's official rails, supports a defined local audience, and produces a Goa-specific pilot decision without exposing raw citizen content to the sponsor.

## Do not say

- "Government-approved" or "Goa Police integrated."
- "AI detects every scam."
- "We prevent fraud loss" before pilot evidence exists.
- "We use I4C, FRI, bank, police, or telecom intelligence" without a signed integration.
- "Citizen data stays only on-device" because voice transcription and some bounded server processing occur on Chetana's own host.
